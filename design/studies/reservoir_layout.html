The bottle shape, the base dome and the neck are estimates until you measure a real Bisleri bottle. Drain times assume a vented bottle, Cd 0.6, real time. H3 water left is calculated for a 1.5 mm lip at the outlet (thin curved inner flange). Fuselage depth = belly (below the closed valve) to the wing underside at the CG.
