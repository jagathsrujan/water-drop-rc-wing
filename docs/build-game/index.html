    How to play

    1. Each step of the real build is one level. Read the step text above the bench.
    2. Parts: click a part card. Check its engraved arrow against the big arrow beside it (nose, up or leading edge) and the engraving: if the text is mirrored and faint, the engraved face is down. Use Turn 180° and Flip over until it is right.
    3. Then click the dashed outline in the view where the part goes (or Place it when the step has no outlines, e.g. laminating on the board).
    4. Actions (pins, glue, checks) are buttons. Some are bad habits: the game tells you why.
    5. The game checks: wrong part, wrong side (L/R), wrong place, wrong orientation, wrong order, forgotten jig or pin, glue on a hinge gap, pushrod not at neutral. Each mistake costs 5 points and goes into the mistake log with the fix.
    6. +10 for every correct move, +25 for a step with no mistakes. Hint costs 2.

    Your progress is saved in this browser. Everything works offline: open docs/build-game/index.html from the repository.
