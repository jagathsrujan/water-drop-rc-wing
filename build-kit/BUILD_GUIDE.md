# Build guide

The whole build in the order of the build game (https://jagathsrujan.github.io/water-drop-rc-wing/build-game/). Generated from the same step data, so the game and this guide always agree. Tick each box. Every laser part has its name engraved; arrows: `< FWD` to the nose, `UP ^` up, `< LE` to the leading edge.

## 1. Laminate the right side layers

*Pod*

The right side is two layers: an outer 1.5 mm balsa skin (grain along the pod, lower and upper strips joined at Z -40) and an inner 1.0 mm layer (grain vertical, pieces no wider than 100 mm). Every joint in one layer is bridged by the other layer. The nose (N), middle (M) and tail-cone (T) panels are separate: they meet at the kinks later. Lay each piece engraved face UP, arrow pointing to the nose ('< FWD'): the engraved faces end up inside the pod.

1. [ ] Lay wax paper (or cling film) on the flat building board
2. [ ] `RO-NL` (balsa, 1.5 mm, `balsa-medium_1.5mm_sheet05.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
3. [ ] `RO-NU` (balsa, 1.5 mm, `balsa-medium_1.5mm_sheet11.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
4. [ ] `RO-M1L` (balsa, 1.5 mm, `balsa-medium_1.5mm_sheet04.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
5. [ ] `RO-M1U` (balsa, 1.5 mm, `balsa-medium_1.5mm_sheet11.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
6. [ ] `RO-MP` (birch ply, 1.5 mm, `birch-ply_1.5mm_sheet01.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
7. [ ] `RO-M3L` (balsa, 1.5 mm, `balsa-medium_1.5mm_sheet04.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
8. [ ] `RO-M3U` (balsa, 1.5 mm, `balsa-medium_1.5mm_sheet07.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
9. [ ] `RO-TL` (balsa, 1.5 mm, `balsa-medium_1.5mm_sheet04.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
10. [ ] `RO-TU` (balsa, 1.5 mm, `balsa-medium_1.5mm_sheet07.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
11. [ ] Spread a thin coat of PVA (Fevicol SH thinned about 10 % with water) over the outer layer
12. [ ] `RI-N1` (balsa, 1 mm, `balsa-medium_1mm_sheet01.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
13. [ ] `RI-N2` (balsa, 1 mm, `balsa-medium_1mm_sheet03.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
14. [ ] `RI-M1` (balsa, 1 mm, `balsa-medium_1mm_sheet04.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
15. [ ] `RI-M2U` (balsa, 1 mm, `balsa-medium_1mm_sheet02.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
16. [ ] `RI-M2G` (birch ply, 1 mm, `birch-ply_1mm_sheet01.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
17. [ ] `RI-M3U` (balsa, 1 mm, `balsa-medium_1mm_sheet04.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
18. [ ] `RI-M3G` (birch ply, 1 mm, `birch-ply_1mm_sheet01.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
19. [ ] `RI-M4` (balsa, 1 mm, `balsa-medium_1mm_sheet02.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
20. [ ] `RI-M5` (balsa, 1 mm, `balsa-medium_1mm_sheet06.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
21. [ ] `RI-T1` (balsa, 1 mm, `balsa-medium_1mm_sheet01.dxf`), arrow `< FWD`
22. [ ] `SD-R` (birch ply, 3 mm, `birch-ply_3mm_sheet01.dxf`)
23. [ ] Cover with wax paper, a flat board and weights; leave it flat overnight

**Do not:**

- *Glue the layers with thin CA and press them by hand* - CA grabs in seconds, before the layers lie flat, and a CA laminate is hard and brittle. Use thinned PVA under weights on a flat board: it gives you time to line the pieces up and dries flat.
- *Turn the inner pieces so their grain runs along the pod like the outer skin* - The pieces are cut with the grain already set: outer skin along X, inner layer vertical. Turning them puts both grains the same way and the side loses its cross-laminate stiffness. Lay each piece as cut, with its engraved arrow to the nose.

Tip: Weigh the 1.0 mm and 1.5 mm sheets before cutting: the light ones go to the inner layer.

## 3. Key the jig base together

*Pod*

The five printed segments JB-A to JB-D2 make one 808 mm base. Its top IS the building surface (Z -117, the underside of the bay floor). Every pod jig is located on it by two 6 mm pins, and each jig's name is engraved where it goes. Arrows '< FWD' point to the nose.

1. [ ] `JB-A` (PLA), arrow `< FWD`
2. [ ] `JB-B` (PLA), arrow `< FWD`
3. [ ] `JB-C` (PLA), arrow `< FWD`
4. [ ] `JB-D1` (PLA), arrow `< FWD`
5. [ ] `JB-D2` (PLA), arrow `< FWD`
6. [ ] Check the base lies flat on the table, then put one drop of thin CA on each dovetail key

**Do not:**

- *Screw the segments to the building board instead of keying them* - Screws pull the thin base out of flat and the segments drift apart: the jig positions are only right when the dovetails are fully home. Key the dovetails on a flat table and fix each with a drop of CA.

Tip: Print the base holes at 6.1 mm and the pins at 5.9 mm. A loose pin: a drop of CA in the base hole.

3. [ ] Left aileron hinges (HINGE-AIL-L1..L4)

5. [ ] Rudder hinges (HINGE-RUD-1..3)
6. [ ] Check a 0.5 mm hinge gap and full throw both ways on every surface
7. [ ] Deflect each surface fully, put 2-3 drops of thin CA on each hinge face (top and bottom); let it wick into the slit

**Do not:**

- *Run a bead of CA along the hinge gap* - CA in the gap glues the surface solid: the hinge cannot bend and the film round it tears. Drops on the hinge faces only, surface deflected, so the CA wicks into the slit, not the gap.
- *Glue the hinges with the surfaces at neutral and full of CA* - Flooding the hinge at neutral glues the pivot line itself. Deflect the surface while the CA wicks; a few drops per hinge face is enough.
