## Notes
- **Balsa grade:** the design needs light 1.5 mm sheets for the D-box (13.5 g or less for each sheet) and 1 hard 3 mm sheet (48 g or more) for the spar caps. An online order cannot select by weight. Ask the shop to select the sheets, or buy 1 or 2 more 1.5 mm sheets (₹248 each) and weigh them when they arrive.
- **Glue:** the three pages have only thick CA and 5-minute epoxy. The build needs thin CA (ribs, hinges) and slow epoxy (carbon caps), so buy those 2 items from another shop (section 2).
- **Film mass:** the mass table uses 50 g/m². The weight of this EMAX-type film is not given. Weigh a 100 × 100 mm piece: each extra 10 g/m² adds about 6 g to the wing.
